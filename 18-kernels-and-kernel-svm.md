## Prep questions

Answer these before lecture; they're the ones that tend to decide whether the lecture lands.

- [ ] The kernel trick: why does the dual only ever need inner products, and what does that buy you?
- [ ] Mercer's condition — what makes a function a valid kernel? (Positive semi-definiteness, back to linear algebra.)
- [ ] Polynomial vs RBF kernels: what does the RBF gamma control, and what does gamma -> infinity do to the decision boundary?
- [ ] Why can an RBF kernel represent an infinite-dimensional feature map without ever computing it?
