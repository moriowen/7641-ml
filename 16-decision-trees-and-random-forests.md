## Prep questions

Answer these before lecture; they're the ones that tend to decide whether the lecture lands.

- [ ] Split criteria: information gain, gain ratio, Gini. Compute each on a small table by hand.
- [ ] Why do unpruned trees overfit, and what are the pruning / stopping options?
- [ ] Bagging vs random forests: RF adds feature subsampling on top of bootstrap sampling. Why does that decorrelate the trees?
- [ ] Out-of-bag error — why is it a free validation estimate?
