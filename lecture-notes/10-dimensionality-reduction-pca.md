## Prep questions

Answer these before lecture; they're the ones that tend to decide whether the lecture lands.

- [ ] PCA two ways: maximum variance and minimum reconstruction error — show they give the same answer.
- [ ] Relationship between the covariance eigen-decomposition and the SVD of the centered data matrix.
- [ ] Why centering matters, and when standardizing (not just centering) is required.
- [ ] PCA vs LDA: unsupervised variance vs supervised class separation. When does PCA actively hurt classification?
