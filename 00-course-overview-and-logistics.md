## Notes on this topic

Course objectives, straight from the [course overview](https://mahdi-roozbahani.github.io/CS46417641-fall2026/docs/course-info/course-overview/):

1. **Basic math for data science and ML** — linear algebra, probability & statistics, information theory, optimization
2. **Unsupervised ML for data exploration** — clustering analysis, dimensionality reduction, kernel density estimation
3. **Supervised learning for predictive analysis** — tree-based models, SVMs, linear classification and regression, neural networks

Prereqs: basic probability/statistics/linear algebra, plus basic Python.

> CSE students: CS 7641 does **not** substitute for CSE 6740, and you cannot get credit for both.
