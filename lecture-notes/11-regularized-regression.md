## Prep questions

Answer these before lecture; they're the ones that tend to decide whether the lecture lands.

- [ ] Ridge vs Lasso: why does the L1 constraint region produce exactly-zero coefficients and the L2 one not?
- [ ] Ridge has a closed form ((X'X + lambda*I)^-1 X'y); Lasso does not. Why?
- [ ] Bayesian reading: ridge = Gaussian prior, lasso = Laplace prior on the weights.
- [ ] How is lambda selected in practice, and why must that happen on validation rather than test data?
