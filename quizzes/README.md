## Mechanics

Per the syllabus (Spring 2026 pattern — confirm on Canvas): open-note, Honorlock-proctored, **five multiple-choice questions, six minutes** for graduate students. Released **Friday 5 PM ET**, due **Monday 11:59 PM ET**. Do the warmup (Q0) before the first graded quiz.
