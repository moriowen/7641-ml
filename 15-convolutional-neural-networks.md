## Prep questions

Answer these before lecture; they're the ones that tend to decide whether the lecture lands.

- [ ] Output-size arithmetic: given input size, kernel, stride, and padding, compute the output shape. Expect this on a quiz.
- [ ] Parameter sharing and local connectivity — how many parameters does a conv layer have vs a dense layer on the same input?
- [ ] What do pooling layers buy, and what do they cost?
- [ ] Transfer learning: which layers do you freeze and which do you retrain, and why?
